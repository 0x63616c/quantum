Install on your Mac

  1. Open the downloaded Voxelite.dmg.
  2. Drag Voxelite to Applications.

First launch

This build isn't notarized yet. In Applications, right-click (Control-click) Voxelite and choose Open, then Open again.

On newer macOS, if that option isn't offered, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
