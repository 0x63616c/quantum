Install on your Mac

  1. Open the downloaded Voxelite.dmg.
  2. Drag Voxelite to Applications.
  3. Open Voxelite from Applications.

First launch and updates

Voxelite is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Open it normally from Applications.

Starting with version 0.3.0, Voxelite checks for updates automatically and installs them with your approval.
