Quantum for macOS (Apple silicon).

1. Download **Quantum.dmg** and open it.
2. Drag **Quantum** into **Applications**.
3. Open **Quantum** from Applications.

Quantum is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. No Gatekeeper workaround is needed.

Starting with version 0.3.0, Quantum checks for updates automatically through Sparkle and prompts you to install them.
