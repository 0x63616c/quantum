# Quantum

Quantum is an Aseprite-style 3D voxel editor for humans and AI agents, with a
Rust/GPUI native macOS shell, `.voxl` projects, GLB/PNG exports and MCP workflows.
The marketing site explains the product and provides downloads, documentation
and integrations. The app keeps artwork central and editing keyboard-friendly.

Verify current behavior in source and manifests. [Product direction](docs/adr/0003-product-direction.md)
and the [glossary](docs/glossary.md) distinguish implementation from deferred
features. Future SwiftUI/macOS work follows the same brand, not an existing
shipping SwiftUI implementation. [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) pins design authority.
